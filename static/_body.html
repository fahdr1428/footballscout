Rank the pool on one metric

Percentiles are against his own position, so a defender and a forward are never ranked on the same scale. Click a row to make him the subject of Similar players.

How similarity is calculated. Each player is a vector of his position's metrics, standardised so every metric counts equally, and similarity is the cosine between two such vectors × 100. A 90% match means the two profiles point in nearly the same direction across all of them. Comparisons only happen inside a position, because a centre-back and a winger share no meaningful axis.
What the money means. A figure in euros is Transfermarkt's market value estimate for that season - not a fee anybody paid. A figure in pounds with an asterisk is the fantasy game's price, a popularity signal set by its operator, not a valuation at all.
What is not guessed. A season that did not measure something shows a dash, not a zero and not an average: no invented ages, heights or tackles. Each season lists what it lacks above.
Sharing a view. The address bar tracks the dataset, the player, the comparison and the tab, so Copy link hands someone the exact screen you are looking at.
