How similarity is calculated

Each player is a vector of his position's metrics, standardised against his peers; similarity is the cosine between two such vectors × 100. Each metric counts by how well it repeats from one season to the next, and players are only compared inside a position.

What the money means

A figure in euros is Transfermarkt's market value estimate - not a fee anybody paid. A figure in pounds with an asterisk is the fantasy game's price, a popularity signal rather than a valuation.

What is never guessed

A season that did not measure something shows a dash, not a zero or an average: no invented ages, heights or tackles. Each season says what it does not measure, under the data picker.

Methodology and data

Who Plays Like Him finds footballers whose statistical profile matches another's, inside the same position, and shows the arithmetic behind every number. It is free, has no sign-in, sends nothing anywhere (your shortlist stays in your browser) and is open source.

Where the numbers come from

How the scores work

  • Percentile - where he ranks against every player in his position in that season and source, 0 to 100. Where a scout wants a number low (errors, fouls), it is flipped so high is always good.
  • Similarity - the cosine between two players' standardised profiles × 100. Each metric counts by how well it repeats from one season to the next (its correlation with the same player's figure a year later, squared), so a metric that is mostly one season's noise barely moves a match. Club readings - goals conceded, clean sheets - count at the median.
  • Rating - the weighted average of his category percentiles, using his position's default weights (the same ones the full app's role fit uses), with the breakdown shown beside it. A category the source does not measure at all is left out and the rest re-weighted; a gap in one player's record counts as average (50). It ranks him against his peers in that league pool and season; it does not adjust for league strength.
  • Price - Transfermarkt's market value, an estimate rather than a fee anyone paid: for each season, the valuation in force on 1 July as it closed; "now" is his latest valuation and its date.

What it cannot tell you

  • Statistics describe output, not decision-making, temperament or injury history.
  • The Top leagues source measures attacking output only - no defending.
  • A season in progress is a few rounds of data: rates swing hard on small samples.
  • Similar styles in different leagues are not equal standards; filter by league to compare like with like.

Jargon buster

How the data is checked

The feeds behind this page count the same matches independently, so the same player-season should carry the same numbers in each. Joined on name, season and league - only names that appear once on both sides, past 900 minutes in both:

  • Nearly every disagreement is a single goal. Two independent xG models - Understat's and the one FBref publishes - correlate at 0.993 for the same players.
  • Assists are not used as a check: Transfermarkt and the fantasy game define them their own way (the fantasy game credits winning a penalty, for one), so they match Understat's only 64-80% of the time, against 97% between Understat and FBref.
  • Transfermarkt's prices and ages are joined by name, club, season and minutes and cover 99.3% of the minutes played; a date of birth implying an impossible age withdraws the match instead of keeping it.
  • Similarity is computed in your browser from the same vectors and weights as the Python engine, and reproduces its numbers: across 1,200 pairs checked in a browser, 0.07 of a point apart on average and never more than 0.4. Reproduce with python scripts/check_parity.py.
  • Every weekly refresh runs the test suite and these checks, and refuses to publish if a finished season lost players. Reproduce the table with python scripts/cross_check.py.

Keeping it current

A scheduled job on GitHub re-fetches Understat's season tables and the Transfermarkt dataset every week and rebuilds this page; the date each source was last refreshed is shown under the data picker. Corrections and suggestions: open an issue.
