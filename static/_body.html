Your shortlist —

How similarity is calculated. Each player is a vector of his position's metrics, standardised against his peers, and similarity is the cosine between two such vectors × 100. A 90% match means the two profiles point in nearly the same direction. Each metric counts by how well it repeats from one season to the next - its correlation with the same player's figure a year later, squared - so a centre-back's aerial duels, which repeat even when he changes club, outweigh his errors, which barely repeat at all. Comparisons only happen inside a position, because a centre-back and a winger share no meaningful axis.
What the money means. A figure in euros is Transfermarkt's market value estimate for that season - not a fee anybody paid. A figure in pounds with an asterisk is the fantasy game's price, a popularity signal set by its operator, not a valuation at all.
What is not guessed. A season that did not measure something shows a dash, not a zero and not an average: no invented ages, heights or tackles. Each season lists what it lacks above.
Sharing a view. The address bar tracks the dataset, the player, the comparison, the tab and the Explorer's axes, so Copy link hands someone the exact screen you are looking at; the Shortlist's link hands them your players. Your shortlist and notes stay in this browser.
